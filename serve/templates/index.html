Method notes
  1. One leaf, centred and filling most of the frame.
  2. Plain background where possible — soil, a hand, or paper.
  3. Even daylight. No harsh shadow falling across the blade.
  4. Focus on the lesion if there is one.

Research prototype. Trained on 2,152 laboratory images of potato leaves. Accuracy on real field photographs is unverified — and measuring it is precisely what this tool collects data for. The percentage shown is a relative model score, not a calibrated probability. Not a substitute for agronomic advice. Uploaded images are stored for evaluation.
